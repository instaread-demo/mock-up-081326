North Bay Police Service's Emergency Response Team deployed on Wednesday night

Emergency Response Unit sent out to a call on Wednesday night for, at this moment, an undisclosed reason

Author of the article:
Greg Estabrooks
Published Aug 12, 2026  •  Last updated 7 hours ago  •  < 1 minute read

Details are very limited, but the Emergency Response Team (ERT) of the North Bay Police Service was deployed to the Sylvan Crescent area on Wednesday night at about 9 pm.  A resident sent the Nugget these shots of some of the events as they happened.

“We heard the siren and by the time I got up to see, the neighborhood was filled with police.  They went in through the front door with a ram and a flash bang, it was pretty impressive,” says Andy Scott.
