About Lumos

Lumos is a pioneering force in the realm of identity management, offering the first Autonomous Identity Platform that not only identifies access risks but also proactively addresses them. Founded in 2022, Lumos has rapidly expanded, serving major clients like GitHub, MongoDB, and Pinterest. The company has grown from 20 to approximately 130 employees, raising over $65 million from prominent investors. This full-time, remote role welcomes candidates from anywhere within ±4 hours of Pacific Time. Lumos offers a flexible work environment, encouraging innovation and collaboration across time zones.

What Your Day Will Look Like

As a Professional Services Project Coordinator, daily tasks include maintaining project plans, ensuring timely updates, and managing the Professional Services Projects Dashboard. The role requires tracking deliverables, coordinating project calls, and escalating potential risks early. This position involves using AI tools to streamline reporting and project management tasks, ensuring efficient and effective service delivery.

Responsibilities & Expectations

  • Maintain Plans: Update project schedules and documents
  • Track Deliverables: Ensure commitments are met
  • Coordinate Calls: Set agendas and manage discussions
  • Escalate Risks: Identify and report potential issues
  • Use AI Tools: Automate reporting tasks

Relevant Experience & Skills Required

  • Education Requirements: No degree required
  • Organizational Skills: Detail-oriented and proactive
  • Tool Proficiency: Project tracking and AI tools
  • Accountability: Ensure commitments are upheld
  • Communication: Clear and effective with teams

Compensation & Benefits

The compensation for this role is $117,000 – $132,000/yr..

Because you are applying directly through the employer’s Applicant Tracking System, your resume needs to be optimized for their software:

  • Make sure the words “Project Coordinator,” “AI Tools,” and “Professional Services” appear in your past experience if applicable.
  • Highlight any specific experience you have with project management tools.
  • Ensure your resume clearly states that you are looking for Full-Time work, so the recruiter knows you are aligned with the role.
