This watermelon mojito is a blend of white rum, mint, lime, watermelon juice and club soda, all combined to make a refreshing beverage. The perfect cocktail for a warm day!

You can’t go wrong with cold drink recipes during the summer months. Some of my favorite options include a blue margarita, mango mojito, Mexican mule and this colorful and delicious watermelon mojito.

When it’s hot outside I always make sure to have plenty of drinks on hand to beat the heat. This watermelon mojito combines a classic summer fruit with plenty of mint for a fresh and flavorful offering!

Watermelon Mojito Ingredients

To make this watermelon mojito, you will need sugar, water, lime juice, mint, white rum, club soda and watermelon juice. You can also add garnishes such as fresh watermelon slices, mint sprigs and lime slices.

How Do You Make A Watermelon Mojito?

Place sugar in a bowl with hot water and stir to make a simple syrup. Pour the syrup into a glass and add the mint leaves. Use a muddler to crush the mint. Add ice on top of the mint mixture, then pour in watermelon juice, lime juice, rum and club soda. Stir gently to combine, then add garnishes and serve.

Tips For The Perfect Drink

  • You will need to use a muddler tool to pulverize the mint leaves for the most authentic flavor. Muddlers can be purchased at kitchen supply stores or online.
  • This recipe makes one watermelon mojito. You can change the number of servings in the recipe card to suit the size of your group and the ingredient amounts will scale up accordingly.
  • I find it easiest to use store bought refrigerated watermelon juice. You can also puree 1 cup of watermelon chunks in a blender and use that instead. If you use watermelon puree, you may want to put it through a strainer before you proceed with the recipe.
  • Use freshly squeezed lime juice for the best flavor.

Quick Tip

For an elegant presentation, dip the rim of your glass in water, then place the rim of the glass on a plate of sugar to get a frosted look.

Recipe FAQs

What does a watermelon mojito contain?

A watermelon mojito contains watermelon juice, rum, club soda, mint, sugar and lime juice.

What alcohol goes best with watermelon?

Watermelon pairs well with alcohol options such as rum, vodka and tequila.

What’s the difference between a mojito and virgin mojito?

A regular mojito contains alcohol, most commonly rum. A virgin mojito does not have alcohol in it.

Flavor Variations

This recipe is delicious as-is, but you can easily switch up some of the ingredients to customize the flavors to your taste.

  • Fruit: Swap out the watermelon juice for a different type of juice such as mango nectar, pomegranate juice, passion fruit nectar or cranberry juice.
  • Club Soda: You can use lime flavored sparkling water instead of club soda.
  • Alcohol: No white rum on hand? Try using vodka, or a flavored rum such as Malibu for a different take on this drink.

Watermelon Mojito

Print Pin Save Saved
AuthorSara Welch
This watermelon mojito is a blend of fresh mint, lime juice, sugar, watermelon juice and club soda. It's a unique cocktail that is so refreshing and also easy to make!
Time
Prep Time5 minutes minutes
Total Time5 minutes minutes
Course Drink
Cuisine Cuban
Serves 1

Ingredients 

  • 1 tablespoon hot water
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 8 mint leaves
  • 1½ ounces white rum
  • 1 ounce lime juice
  • 3½ ounces watermelon juice or watermelon puree
  • 2 ounces club soda
  • mint sprigs, watermelon slices and lime wedges for garnish

Instructions 

  • Place the sugar and hot water in a bowl. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
  • Place the mint leaves and sugar mixture in the bottom of a glass. Use a muddler and muddle everything together until the mint leaves are crushed.
  • Add ice on top of the sugar and mint mixture. Pour in the rum, lime juice and watermelon juice.
  • Pour in the club soda, then stir to combine.
  • Garnish with lime wedges, watermelon slices and mint sprigs if desired, then serve.

Notes

  1. You will need to use a muddler tool to pulverize the mint leaves for the most authentic flavor. Muddlers can be purchased at kitchen supply stores or online.
  2. This recipe makes one watermelon mojito. You can change the number of servings in the recipe card to suit the size of your group and the ingredient amounts will scale up accordingly.
  3. I find it easiest to use store bought refrigerated watermelon juice. You can also puree 1 cup of watermelon chunks in a blender and use that instead. Strain the puree before you add it to your drink.
