3 Birth Months That Need Public Displays of Affection To Feel Loved

Public displays of affection (PDA!) is a funny thing, some people love it, while others hate it. The public romantic spotlight is not for everyone, but there are some birth months that need the attention. Read on to find out the three birth months who need the most PDA:

April 

April, especially April women, love PDA. Those born in April are bold and they are one of the most confident birth months of the year. April natives are extraordinarily passionate too. They give their all in everything they do, making every love story exciting and memorable with their fiery energy. In a relationship, someone born in April wants everyone to know they are with their partner. As such, they want their partner to give them constant love and affection. Further, April are fearless and self-assured, which fuels their ambition and helps them achieve their wildest dreams. This confidence not only inspires their professional and personal pursuits, but also their needs in a relationship. Someone born in April will confidently show their feelings and affections to their partner and require their partner to do the same. As a whole, April are uninhibited and truly authentically themselves. 

March

March birth months are intuitive, creative, and have a wide capacity for empathy, but they also are sensitive, compassionate, and incredibly loving. March have a reputation for being a hopelessly romantic partner. They love public displays of affection because they have so many emotions and feelings to share. Further, they also have a soft spot for partners who aren’t afraid of their emotions and embrace their enthusiasm for possibility. They love when their partners wrap their arms around them and show them love. March love to connect with their partner on an emotional and physical level, which is why they need their partner to express their love in all settings. Given that March want to be close to their partners at all times, they need little affectionate gestures in public. 

June 

June birth months are quick-witted, excitable, and dynamic. Further, some of June’s greatest strengths include their adaptability, their curiosity, their memory skills, and their intellectual stamina. They are social and love to be around others. In a relationship, those born in June are oftentimes consumed by the desire to connect with their partner. This is why those born in June crave a partner who will express love and affection to them at all times. June-born individuals want to know that their partner is around and they often feel a burst of energy when they receive these displays of public affection. These June signs enjoy having a good time and need a partner who wants to confidently and excitedly shower them with love.
