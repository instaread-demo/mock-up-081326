UPDATED: Two women charged with murder following Regina double homicide

By

Last Updated: August 12, 2026 at 4:40 p.m.

Two women are facing murder charges in relation to a double homicide which Regina police discovered Tuesday morning.

Patricia Stevenson, 19, appeared in court Wednesday morning. Charges were announced against Rose Sparvier, 42, Wednesday afternoon.

As Stevenson was brought into a prisoner’s box at Regina provincial court, a woman in the gallery began loudly weeping and groaning.

Stevenson, a small woman, said to the older woman through the glass: “I’m sorry mom, I love you.”

The woman in the gallery continued to show emotion as Judge Marylynne Beaton read out the charges.

Stevenson stands charged that on Aug. 11 she murdered Sarah McGillis and Arland Nahnepowisk, whose names were read out publicly in open court by Beaton.

The accused teen also faces weapons charges.

Sparvier faces two counts of second-degree murder, but not weapons charges. She was scheduled to attend court Wednesday afternoon.

Prosecutor Ethan Reis told Beaton the Crown is opposed to Stevenson’s release. As such, she will remain in custody for the time being.

“Are you wanting a lawyer to help you?” the judge asked.

“Yes,” Stevenson replied.

Legal Aid Saskatchewan lawyer Tarim Ansari said a publicly funded trial lawyer would be assigned to Stevenson and requested that the matter be adjourned to a date later in August.

As Stevenson was being led out of the courtroom, the older woman in the gallery urged her to “pray, pray.”

Stevenson touched the glass from the inside of the prisoner’s box, and the older woman touched the glass from the outside.

As they tried to exchange words, Stevenson was led away, and the older woman said: “I love you.”

According to police, at around 4:30 a.m. Tuesday, officers were sent to the 1100 block of Elphinstone Street for a report that two people had been shot.

When they arrived, they found a 42-year-old woman and a 37-year-old man dead outside a home, according to a news release, which goes on to state that Stevenson fled the scene and attempted to hide in a nearby yard.

“She was taken into custody with the assistance of the Canine Unit. During the arrest, the Police Service Dog bit the suspect,” states the release, adding that she was taken to hospital.

The investigation is ongoing, according to police.
