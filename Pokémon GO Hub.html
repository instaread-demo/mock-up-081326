The Pokémon GO 10th Anniversary celebrations just got even bigger! Trainers worldwide will be able to claim a free Timed Research culminating in an encounter with a guaranteed 100% IV Mewtwo featuring a Special Background.

The Timed Research will be available from Wednesday, August 12 at 4:00 p.m. PDT until Sunday, September 6 at 11:59 p.m. local time. As an added bonus, this special Mewtwo will already have Mega Level 3 unlocked for Mega Mewtwo Y!

10th Anniversary Celebration Timed Research for Hundo Mewtwo

Complete the Timed Research to encounter a 100% IV Mewtwo with a Special Background and the third Mega Level unlocked for Mega Mewtwo Y, the strongest Psychic type in Pokémon GO.

10th Anniversary Celebration (1/1)

Task Rewards
Catch 10 Pokémon 1× Master Ball
Power up Pokémon 10 times 500× Mewtwo Mega Energy

Rewards

  • Mewtwo 100 IV

Please note that Timed Research expires. The tasks associated with this Timed Research must be completed, and their rewards must be claimed before Sunday, September 6, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time.

Note

  • Trainers who caught a Mewtwo at the in-person Times Square event on July 9 will receive a Mewtwo with different stats and different rewards.
  • Mewtwo with a Times Square background will be available again at some point in the future.

Guaranteed Mewtwo In-Game

Celebrate 10 Years of Pokémon GO

As part of the 10th anniversary celebration, Trainers around the world will be able to enjoy free Timed Research leading to an encounter with a Mewtwo with maxed-out stats and a Special Background. Mewtwo caught from the Timed Research will also have the third Mega Level unlocked for Mega Mewtwo Y.

We’re excited to mark this milestone by sharing this special reward with Trainers everywhere. Let’s celebrate the journey we’ve taken together over the past 10 years!

Please note that Timed Research expires. The tasks associated with this Timed Research must be completed and their rewards must be claimed before Sunday, September 6, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time.
