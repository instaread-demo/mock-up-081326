Talladega County Sheriff’s Office seeks public’s help identifying person in felony probe

Mon, July 20, 2026 by SylacaugaNews.com Digital Team
SHARE NOW
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:45
Instaread

SYLACAUGA, Ala – The Talladega County Sheriff’s Office is asking for the public’s help in identifying a person connected to an ongoing felony investigation.

According to the sheriff’s office, the incident occurred on Friday, July 17 at the Walco Handi-Mart in Sylacauga. Investigators have issued a be-on-the-lookout order, commonly known as a BOLO, and are seeking to identify the individual shown in a released photograph.

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Talladega County Sheriff’s Office Investigations Division at (256) 761-2141.

Anonymous tips may also be submitted through the sheriff’s office website or by calling Crime Stoppers at 215-STOP.

Authorities said tips that lead to the identification and location of the suspect may be eligible for a cash reward.
