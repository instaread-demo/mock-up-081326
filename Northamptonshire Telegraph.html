Four crews called to 'large' fire in Northamptonshire village

By William Carter
Published 12th Aug 2026, 15:48 BST

Firefighters have been called to a large fire which has spread to grassland in Main Road, Grendon.

Crews are currently at the scene, where the blaze has spread across 20 acres of grassland close to Main Road in the village.

A Northants Fire and Rescue spokesman said: “Northamptonshire Fire and Rescue Service currently has four fire engines tackling a large fire on Main Road in Grendon after being called shortly before 2pm today (Wednesday, August 12).

“Firefighters are working to put out the fire, which has spread to approximately 20 acres of grassland.

“Nearby residents that are affected by smoke are advised to please keep their windows and doors shut.”
