Ten winners will each receive 30 cases of Alani Nu Witch’s Brew Energy, a mini fridge and more. The $1,370 package stands out for its large drink supply and seasonal extras.

Each prize also includes one case of Voodoo Vanilla Energy Elixir, one Potion Pack case and a Witch’s Brew merchandise box. Altogether, the sweepstakes will award $13,700 in prizes.

How to enter the Alani Nu Witch’s Brew giveaway

This is a social-post giveaway hosted on Instagram. To submit the standard entry:

  1. Follow the official @AlaniNutrition account.
  2. Like the giveaway post.
  3. Tag a friend who also follows the account.

You can earn one bonus entry by sharing the post to your Instagram Story. The limit is two entries total, so sharing it more than once will not create additional eligible entries.

The promotion is open to residents of the United States and Washington, D.C., who are at least 18 and have reached the age of majority where they live. A valid government-issued photo ID is required.

Enter between noon ET on August 12 and noon ET on August 14, 2026. Winners are expected to be selected in a random drawing after the entry period and contacted through Instagram direct message, so check message requests carefully and confirm that any notification comes from the official account.
