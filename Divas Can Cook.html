Here’s What You Need

INGREDIENTS

  • all-purpose flour
  • baking powder
  • salt
  • granulated sugar
  • salted butter, very cold
  • buttermilk, very cold

How To Make Buttermilk Drop Biscuits Recipe

  1. Preheat the oven to 450 F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  3. Grate the cold butter directly into the flour mixture, then gently toss to coat the butter pieces.
  4. Use a pastry cutter to cut the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse, large crumbs.
  5. Gently stir in the buttermilk just until a wet, sticky dough forms and no dry flour remains. Do not overmix.
  6. Drop 1/4-cup portions of dough on the prepared baking sheet, placing them about 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake on the top rack for 12-13 minutes, or until the biscuits are puffed and cooked through.
  8. Remove the biscuits from the oven and carefully pull the parchment paper out from underneath.
  9. Set the oven to broil. Return the biscuits to the oven and broil just until the tops are lightly golden. Keep a close eye on the biscuits the entire time, since they can brown quickly.
  10. Remove the biscuits from the oven and immediately brush the tops with melted butter. Serve warm..

Mo’s Recipe Tips

Keep ingredients cold. Use cold butter and buttermilk. The biscuit dough should be cold when going into the oven, and using cold ingredients helps. This is key to creating fluffy, tender drop biscuits.

Use all-purpose flour. I love King Arthur all-purpose flour. I’d skip White Lily flour for these biscuits.

Measure flour correctly. Spoon the flour into the measuring cups and level it off.

Use a good butter. Since butter flavors these biscuits so much, it’s worth using a good brand. I like Kerry Gold brand because it gives such a beautiful yellow color and a rich buttery flavor.

Don’t overmix the dough. Stir just until the ingredients are combined. Overmixing will create a dry dough.

Rustic-looking dough is perfect. Drop biscuit dough is soft, thick, sticky, and lumpy. They should look a bit messy and rustic, not smooth like rolled biscuit dough.

Brush them with butter. As soon as the biscuits come out of the oven, brush them with melted salted butter.

Change It Up!

I love that these delicious fluffy drop biscuits can be changed up so many ways. Be creative but try to keep the add-ins under 1 cup. Here are a few of my favorite add-ins!

  • Bacon cheddar- Add 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese and 1/3 cup of cooked, chopped bacon
  • Blueberry lemon- Add 3/4 cup blueberries and 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest
  • Chocolate chips- Add 1/2 cup of semi-sweet chocolate chips

FAQs

Can I use regular milk instead of buttermilk? Buttermilk gives these biscuits their tender texture and rich flavor. You can use whole milk, but the flavor will be milder. You can create buttermilk by mixing 1 tablespoon of lemon juice with 1 cup of whole milk and letting it sit for 10 minutes.

TIP: Did you know that you can freeze buttermilk cubes? Now you’ll always have some on hand for recipes. Here’s how!

Can I make the dough ahead of time? For the best flavor, bake these biscuits immediately after mixing up the dough. You can, however, mix up the dry ingredients ahead of time and add the butter and buttermilk when ready to bake.

Can I add cheese to these biscuits? Absolutely! Fold the shredded cheese into the dry ingredients. You can also add garlic powder, Cajun seasoning, and chopped herbs for easy cheddar bay biscuits.

Why are my biscuits flat? It could be that you use too much baking powder, a self-rising flour, or your dough was warm going into the oven. Make sure to use the correct amount of baking powder and ensure that it is not expired. Be careful not to overmix the batter and put your biscuits into the oven immediately while the dough is still super cold.

How To Store Drop Biscuits

To store, let the biscuits cool completely and then place them in an airtight container. Keep them at room temperature for 2-3 days or refrigerate for up to 5 days.

Freeze for up to 3 months in a freezer-safe container. Thaw at room temperature and reheat briefly in the microwave.

Buttermilk Drop Biscuits

Fluffy, soft, and tender buttermilk drop biscuits!
Servings: 10
Prep Time: 10 minutes mins
Cook Time: 12 minutes mins
Total Time: 22 minutes mins

Ingredients 

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • ½ cup salted butter, very cold or frozen
  • 1½ cups buttermilk, very cold (May need more or less)
  • 1-2 tablespoons salted butter, melted (for brushing biscuits)

Instructions
 

  • Preheat the oven to 450℉ Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  • In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.

  • Grate the cold butter directly into the flour mixture, then gently toss to coat the butter pieces.

  • Use a pastry cutter to cut the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse, large crumbs.
  • Gently stir in the buttermilk just until a wet, sticky dough forms and no dry flour remains. Do not overmix.
    1½ cups buttermilk, very cold
  • Drop 1/4-cup portions of dough on the prepared baking sheet, placing them about 2 inches apart.
  • Bake on the top rack for 10-12 minutes, or until the biscuits are puffed and cooked through.
  • Remove the biscuits from the oven and carefully pull the parchment paper out from underneath.
  • Set the oven to broil. Return the biscuits to the oven and broil just until the tops are lightly golden. Keep a close eye on the biscuits the entire time, since they can brown quickly.
  • Remove the biscuits from the oven and immediately brush the tops with melted butter. Serve warm.
